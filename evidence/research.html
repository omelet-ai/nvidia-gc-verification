Founder’s Research Track Record & Scientific Foundation

T4 · Research evidence · Jinkyoo Park · KAIST · Six selected co-authored papers

Jinkyoo Park · Omelet co-founder and CEO · KAIST faculty since 2016. The official KAIST profile lists his Industrial & Systems Engineering appointment and research in neural combinatorial optimization, multi-agent reinforcement learning and industrial design optimization.

Six selected co-authored publications are linked below to primary publication records, accessible papers and five author-linked code repositories. They document research expertise relevant to Omelet’s technical direction.

Attribution. Jinkyoo Park appears in the author list of every selected paper. These are collaborative academic publications. The relevance notes are Omelet’s interpretation of the research connection, not evidence that each method is deployed in a customer product or that the company exclusively owns the research.

RL4CO KDD 2025

RL4CO: An Extensive Reinforcement Learning for Combinatorial Optimization Benchmark

Federico Berto et al.; Jinkyoo Park is a listed co-author.

Contribution: A shared research framework spanning 27 combinatorial-optimization environments and 23 baselines, with modular training and evaluation.

Relevance: Reusable optimization infrastructure and systematic comparison of learned solvers.

Publication / author record · Read paper · Source code

The author-maintained arXiv record identifies KDD 2025 Oral and links the ACM DOI. Authorship and the 27/23 counts are visible in the paper.

PARCO NeurIPS 2025

PARCO: Parallel AutoRegressive Models for Multi-Agent Combinatorial Optimization

Federico Berto, Chuanbo Hua, Laurin Luttmann, Jiwoo Son, Junyoung Park, Kyuree Ahn, Changhyun Kwon, Lin Xie, Jinkyoo Park.

Contribution: Parallel solution construction with agent communication and conflict handling for multi-agent routing and scheduling.

Relevance: Coordinated decision-making across multiple vehicles or resources.

Publication / author record · Read paper · Source code

NeurIPS proceedings confirm the conference year and Jinkyoo Park in the author list.

Minsu Kim, Junyoung Park, Jinkyoo Park.

Meta-SAGE ICML 2023

Meta-SAGE: Scale Meta-Learning Scheduled Adaptation with Guided Exploration for Mitigating Scale Shift on Combinatorial Optimization

Jiwoo Son, Minsu Kim, Hyeonah Kim, Jinkyoo Park.

Contribution: Adapts pretrained optimization policies to larger instances using scale-aware representations and guided test-time exploration.

Relevance: Research on scaling learned solvers beyond their training-instance sizes.

Publication / author record · Read paper · Source code

Neuro CROSS-exchange ICLR 2023

Learning to CROSS exchange to solve min-max vehicle routing problems

Minjun Kim, Junyoung Park, Jinkyoo Park.

Contribution: A graph neural network predicts promising route exchanges to guide a conventional search operator for min-max vehicle routing.

Relevance: Hybrid AI and operations-research methods for practical routing decisions.

Publication / author record · Read paper · Conference slides

ICLR confirms authorship and the accepted title above. The linked earlier preprint uses the title “Neuro CROSS exchange: Learning to CROSS exchange to solve realistic vehicle routing problems”.
