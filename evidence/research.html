Research versus commercial evidence

The public paper and repository support the research foundation and its authorship. They do not establish the deployment state of Omelet’s commercial platform or NVIDIA components at any named customer. The repository describes an earlier NeurIPS 2023 GLFrontiers Workshop oral; that distinction should not be silently transferred to KDD 2025.

Publication count method

Count direct entries in the lab page’s ML Conference Papers list; then count their venue lines containing NeurIPS, ICML or ICLR. Workshop and engineering-paper lists are excluded. The live list may change after this dated review.
