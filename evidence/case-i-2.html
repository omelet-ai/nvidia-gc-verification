Reported outcome

−73% stockout rate · −43% waste quantity

Stockout rate: 15% to 4% (11 percentage points; 73.3% relative reduction). Weekly waste quantity per store: 56 to 32 (42.9% lower). These inventory results are distinct from routing cost savings and cuOpt experiments.

Problem and solution

Balance running out against throwing away for short-life dairy.

Present optimal trade-off points between stockout and waste for short-shelf-life items; the operator chooses the final order pattern.

Model / method: AI forecast + MO-MILP.

Comparison and measurement scope

Existing ordering versus the selected stockout/waste policy for short-life products.

Source: Omelet case-study portfolio. Public summary prepared 17 September 2026. Underlying measurement dates, raw logs and detailed customer analysis are confidential. These are customer-project results; NVIDIA cuOpt use is not inferred.

An ordering tool that shows the stockout-vs-waste options so the operator picks a balance and keeps re-ordering to it.
