What the article reports

Rapportian reports that Omelet’s “Ssuk-scheduler” generates a draft nurse roster within 90 seconds and supports conversational revisions. The article describes ongoing validation in a ward at an unnamed Korean university hospital and a planned KHF 2026 demonstration.
