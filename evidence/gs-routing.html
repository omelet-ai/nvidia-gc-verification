How to interpret the results

Gap uses a moving, per-instance best-across-solvers reference. Compare distance across time budgets, not the gap percentages as if they shared a fixed global optimum. Aggregated km and mean per-instance gap may rank differently; raw per-instance records are needed to reconstruct the averages.

The appendix reports 2/3 cuOpt wins at both budgets and run-to-run variation: cuOpt ±1.24 / ±0.50 percentage points and RouteOpt-ALNS ±0.87 / ±1.27 percentage points (10 / 60 seconds). The deck describes the frontier difference as within noise. A formal significance/equivalence test and comparable variation for primary RouteOpt are not supplied; no statistical-tie conclusion is independently verified here.

These are solver distance results. They do not independently prove the separate 20%+ realized delivery-cost reduction claim. Only aggregate results are published; customer orders and routes are not included.
